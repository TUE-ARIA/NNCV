# 5LSM0: Neural Networks for Computer Vision

> [!TIP]
> This repository is also a website, with search and navigation: **[tue-aria.github.io/NNCV](https://tue-aria.github.io/NNCV/)**.

5LSM0 is a master course of the Department of Electrical Engineering at Eindhoven University of Technology, taught by the [Architectures for Reliable Image Analysis Lab](https://github.com/TUE-ARIA). You learn how modern neural networks for computer vision work, from convolutional networks and vision transformers to generative models, and how to train and evaluate them in PyTorch.

## What you find here

| Part | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [**Topics**](topics/README.md) | Lecture notes, slides and an optional notebook with exercises for each of the eleven topics. |
| [**Final assignment**](final-assignment/README.md) | The ATLAS Surgical Video Challenge: a project on surgical video that counts for 50% of your grade. |
